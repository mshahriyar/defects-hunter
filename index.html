09Straight answers

Before you ask.

Why an outside QA team instead of one more developer?
Developers test what they built the way they built it — you can't easily find your own assumption. We arrive without it. And since we have no dev arm, we never grade our own homework.
What do you need from us to start?
Staging access, test accounts, whatever requirements exist (rough is fine), your issue tracker, and one person who can answer questions. Discovery call within 48 hours; pilot can start the following Monday.
Is our code and data safe?
NDA before the first call if you want one. We work in staging with synthetic data and never ask for production database access. HIPAA / GDPR / PCI scope — tell us early, it changes the plan.
Contract terms?
QA retainers: month to month after an initial three-month term, 30 days' notice. Growth needs longer because SEO compounds — three months on Launch, six on Grow. Fixed projects: 50% up front, 50% on delivery.
